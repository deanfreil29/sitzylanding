Dinner’s better with company. New company especially.

Making friends as an adult is hard. Sitzy makes it as easy as saying yes to dinner: a real reservation, a handful of open seats, and a topic that gets everyone talking from the first bite.

How it works

From open seat to old friends

Pick a table

Browse tables near you and find a topic, a time, and a spot that sounds like your kind of night.

Grab a seat

Request to join or hop right in. Once you’re confirmed, say hi to the table in your group chat.

Show up hungry

Meet at the restaurant, talk about something real, and tap “sit again” for anyone you’d love to see next time.

Tables for every kind of talk

Every table has a topic.

A theme means nobody has to wonder what to say. Pick something you love, something you’re curious about, or just something to eat.

Meeting new people, safely

Sitzy tables happen at public restaurants, never private homes. Hosts can approve every guest, profiles carry reviews from past meals, and you can report or block anyone in a tap. Always trust your gut, and if you ever feel unsafe, leave and contact local authorities. In an emergency, call 911.
